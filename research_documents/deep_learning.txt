Deep Learning
Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses multi-layer neural networks to learn hierarchical representations from data.
Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) are commonly used for image recognition. A CNN learns spatial features through convolutional layers.
Recurrent neural networks process sequences, while transformers use attention to model relationships between tokens.
Deep learning often requires substantial training data and computation. Regularization and independent test sets help assess generalization.
